Our Vision

A Connected RPAS Innovation Ecosystem

To establish a connected RPAS innovation ecosystem that accelerates technology adoption, strengthens Canadian industry capabilities, creates high-value employment opportunities, and positions Alberta as a centre for emerging autonomous technologies. The Hub connects industry, emerging technologies, entrepreneurs, and talent to unlock new opportunities.

AACP™ RPAS Innovation, Services & Workforce Hub

How to Join

Your Path into the RPAS Workforce Hub

Cohort spaces are limited. Acceptance is based on AACP™ review of your expression of interest — typically within 5 business days.

01

Submit your expression of interest

Tell us about your background, experience and interest in the RPAS Workforce Hub. There are no formal prerequisites.

02

AACP™ reviews your application

We review your background and interest and respond within 5 business days. We look for genuine curiosity about RPAS career opportunities — no prior experience required.

03

Confirm your place and fee

Individuals whose expressions of interest are accepted will receive next-step instructions, including payment arrangements. Your place is confirmed once the $1,200 CAD program fee has been received.

04

Complete the RPAS Workforce Hub

Career intelligence, applied RPAS learning and industry context — delivered over one structured week.
