Specialized Workforce Pathways

Four Pathways for Aviation Workforce
Transition

The two-week Aviation Workforce Transition Program (AWTP) supports participants across four aviation and aerospace specializations.

Pathway 01

Aircraft Engine Maintenance

Workforce readiness and transition support for individuals with relevant aircraft engine maintenance education, training or experience preparing for employment opportunities in Canada's aviation industry.

Pathway 02

Aircraft Maintenance Engineering (AME)

Workforce readiness and transition support for aviation maintenance students, graduates and experienced professionals preparing to enter or advance within Canada's aviation maintenance workforce.

Pathway 03

Pilot / Flight Operations

Workforce readiness and transition support for individuals with relevant flight training or aviation operations backgrounds preparing for employment and career transitions.

Pathway 04

RPAS / Autonomous Systems

Workforce readiness and transition support for individuals with relevant aviation backgrounds seeking opportunities in RPAS and autonomous aviation operations.

Who Is This Program For?

Designed for Aviation-Trained
Individuals

The AWTP is designed for individuals with relevant aviation education, training or professional experience who are ready to transition into or advance within Canada's aviation workforce.

Aviation Students & Recent Graduates

Currently enrolled in or recently completed relevant aviation education or training — flight training, AME, aerospace engineering or aviation management.

Internationally Trained Aviation Professionals

Individuals with aviation licences, training or professional experience from outside Canada seeking a pathway into the Canadian aviation workforce.

Experienced Aviation Workers

Active or recently lapsed aviation professionals transitioning into new roles, sectors or specializations within Canada's aviation and aerospace industry.

STEM Graduates with Aviation Backgrounds

Engineering, science or technical graduates whose education and experience align with aviation and aerospace workforce needs.
