1. Purpose of Authorization

AACP™ Career Navigator is an educational and career intelligence platform designed to help students explore career opportunities, understand educational requirements and develop personalized career plans.

This form enables parents or legal guardians to provide informed authorization for their child's participation in applicable Career Navigator services.

4. Student Privacy and Data Protection

Career Navigator applies a privacy-by-design approach that minimizes the collection of student personal information.

Depending on the features accessed, the platform may collect:

  • Account identification and email address.
  • Age eligibility information.
  • Grade level.
  • Completed and planned courses.
  • Career interests, strengths and preferences.
  • Saved career plans.
  • Consent and sharing permissions.

Career Navigator does not require student real names, exact dates of birth, Alberta Student Numbers, academic marks or academic transcripts.

Stored application data is hosted in Canada.

5. Platform Participation

Please indicate your authorization for the following:

  • I authorize my child to register and use applicable Career Navigator educational and career exploration features.
  • I authorize my child to create and save personalized career and education plans.

6. ACIA™ Artificial Intelligence Authorization

ACIA™ provides AI-assisted career guidance to support informed career exploration and education planning.

ACIA™ operates through the AACP Intelligence Gateway hosted in Canada.

Student messages, conversation histories and account identifiers are not transmitted to external AI providers.

AI-assisted responses are subject to privacy, safety and content validation.

ACIA™ does not determine academic admission, professional licensing, employment eligibility or suitability for a particular career.

ACIA™ is not a counselling or emergency response service.

Optional authorization:

  • I authorize my child to access ACIA™ AI-assisted career guidance.

Declining this authorization does not prevent access to other approved Career Navigator features that do not require ACIA™.

7. Student-Controlled Sharing

Career Navigator allows students to share their career plans with authorized parents, guardians, teachers and career advisors.

Authorized recipients receive view-only access. Students may revoke access at any time, subject to applicable legal and institutional requirements.

  • I authorize my child to use applicable career plan sharing features.

This authorization does not automatically grant me or any other person access to my child's career plan.

8. Premium Subscriptions and Payments

Career Navigator offers free and paid subscription services.

Parents, guardians and institutions may sponsor a student's Premium subscription.

Authorization to use Career Navigator does not constitute authorization to charge a payment method or initiate a paid subscription.

Any purchase must be separately approved through the applicable payment process.

Payment for a subscription does not automatically grant access to student personal information or career plans.

9. Withdrawal of Authorization

Parents or legal guardians may withdraw applicable authorization by contacting AACP™ or using available authorization management features.

Withdrawal may result in restricted access to features requiring that authorization.

Students may independently delete their accounts through Career Navigator, subject to applicable legal and data retention requirements.

10. Institutional Participation

Where Career Navigator is accessed through a school, school board or other educational institution, additional institutional privacy, consent and authorization requirements may apply.

This form does not replace any mandatory institutional authorization process.
