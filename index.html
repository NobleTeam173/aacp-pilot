Who Is AACP™ For?

AACP Supports People at
Different Stages of Their Journey

AACP™ is designed for people at different points in their aviation and aerospace career journey — from early exploration to career transition.

Emerging Talent

Exploring Aviation & Aerospace

Youth, recent graduates and early-career professionals interested in exploring aviation and aerospace career opportunities for the first time.

Aviation & Aerospace Professionals

Already in the Industry

Individuals already working in aviation or aerospace who want to explore career pathways, development opportunities and potential next steps within the industry.

Career Transitioners

Bringing Experience from Another Sector

Experienced professionals from other industries who want to explore how their existing skills and experience could transfer into aviation and aerospace.

Whether you're entering the industry, already working within it, or bringing experience from another sector — AACP™ helps you explore where you could fit and identify your next step.
ACIA — Aviation Career Intelligence

Discover Your Aviation & Aerospace Pathway

ACIA is AACP™'s proprietary career-intelligence experience, helping participants gain personalized insight into their strengths, development opportunities and potential alignment with aviation and aerospace career pathways.

ACIA is completed inside your secure AACP™ participant dashboard. It is part of your AACP™ program experience — not a standalone open registration.

Participant Outcomes

What You Can Gain
from AACP™

AACP™ is designed to support career clarity, industry exposure and workforce readiness — not to guarantee a specific career outcome.

Greater understanding of aviation and aerospace career opportunities across Canada

Insight into personal strengths and areas for development in relation to aviation and aerospace pathways

Exposure to aviation and aerospace professionals and industry environments

Better understanding of education, training and career pathways relevant to your interests and goals

Workforce-readiness development to support your next step in aviation and aerospace

Greater clarity about potential next steps in the aviation and aerospace sector

Connections to relevant industry, training and career opportunities where appropriate

A personalized career intelligence profile to support development planning and career conversations

AACP™ program recognition upon successful completion, where applicable

AACP™ complements formal education, technical training and regulated aviation pathways — helping participants explore opportunities, strengthen workforce readiness and make more informed decisions about their next steps. AACP™ does not replace regulated aviation education, occupational licensing, certification or technical training.
