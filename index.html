Platform Capabilities

Built for the Demands of
High-Stakes Aviation Careers

Every capability is designed around regulatory compliance, rigorous skill validation, and measurable career outcomes for Canada's aviation sector.

01

Competency Validation

A structured, multi-source evidence framework maps candidate performance to CARs regulatory standards through direct observation, coach review, and structured assessment at every stage of a participant's journey.

02

AI Career Intelligence

Personalized career pathway recommendations and role-match scoring powered by a competency index algorithm that accounts for evidence quality, rubric alignment, and individual development trajectory.

03

Cohort Analytics

Purpose-built employer dashboards surface readiness bands, competency gap maps by role family, and workforce trend analysis across the full pilot cohort in a single, actionable view.

04

Evidence Portfolio

Participants build a verifiable digital portfolio of competency evidence across five career pathways. Coaches review, certify, and return structured feedback with full audit traceability.

05

Privacy and Governance

Granular consent management, immutable audit logging, and role-based access control ensure participant data is governed to the highest standard throughout the entire assessment lifecycle.

06

Role Matching Engine

A weighted fit-score algorithm connects job-ready participants to aviation role families including Jet Technician, Avionics Specialist, and AMT with transparent, explainable gap analysis for every match.

Analytics That Drive

Live Readiness Scoring

02

03

Regulatory Compliance Alerts

Automatic identification of CARs-aligned training gaps before they affect a participant's placement eligibility or regulatory standing.
