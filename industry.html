AACP™ partners with employers, educational institutions, communities and industry organizations to develop structured workforce intelligence capacity across Canada's aviation and aerospace sector.

Partnership Opportunities

Who We Partner With

Aviation and aerospace workforce development is most effective when industry, education, and community work from shared intelligence. AACP™ builds the infrastructure that makes that possible.

Employers & Aviation Operators

Access aggregated workforce intelligence on the talent pipeline entering aviation and aerospace. Understand cohort-level capability trends, emerging workforce characteristics and development needs.

  • Aggregated, anonymized cohort workforce intelligence
  • Cohort-level capability trend data
  • Alignment with your workforce planning needs
  • Participation in workforce development curriculum and programming
  • Connection to participants who have expressed interest in industry engagement

Educational Institutions

Strengthen the transition between aviation education and workforce readiness. AACP™ workforce intelligence complements technical training with structured capability assessment and career direction.

  • Structured workforce intelligence for graduates and students
  • Transition support from education to workforce readiness
  • ACIA™ integration pathways for existing programs
  • Employer connection and placement intelligence
  • Curriculum alignment with workforce capability requirements

Industry Organizations & Associations

Aviation and aerospace associations play a critical role in defining workforce standards and advocating for workforce development investment. AACP™ provides the data and intelligence infrastructure to support that work.

  • Sector-level workforce intelligence and capability data
  • Data foundation for workforce development advocacy
  • Alignment with national and provincial workforce strategies
  • Pilot program opportunities for workforce development initiatives
  • Research and reporting collaboration

Communities & Workforce Development Organizations

Aviation and aerospace careers are increasingly accessible to underrepresented communities. AACP™ partners with workforce development organizations to bring structured workforce intelligence to communities building their aviation future.

  • Community-based workforce intelligence programs
  • Accessible pathways into aviation and aerospace careers
  • RPAS Workforce Hub as a community workforce entry point
  • Cohort-based programs for community groups and organizations
  • Collaboration with Indigenous and rural communities
How It Works

The AACP™ Workforce Intelligence Pipeline

AACP™ builds workforce intelligence at every stage — from initial capability discovery through to structured Workforce Profiles that employers and institutions can understand and act on.

01

Participant Discovery

Participants engage with AACP™ through aviation pathways or the RPAS Workforce Hub, expressing interest in aviation and aerospace careers.

02

ACIA™ Assessment

ACIA™ Career Intelligence generates structured Workforce Profiles through a series of scenario-based career intelligence activities.

03

Workforce Profile

Participants receive a personalized Workforce Profile identifying capabilities, development areas and career pathway alignment.

04

Industry Intelligence

Aggregated, anonymized workforce intelligence informs employer pipeline planning, workforce strategy and sector-level reporting.

What AACP™ Brings

Workforce Intelligence Infrastructure

AACP™ is designed to build shared workforce intelligence capacity — not just individual career guidance. Here is what partners gain access to.

01

Structured Workforce Intelligence Data

ACIA™ generates structured Workforce Profiles across a range of career-relevant capability areas — providing a consistent framework for understanding participant strengths and development areas at the cohort level.

02

Pipeline Visibility

Understand who is entering the aviation and aerospace workforce — their backgrounds, capability strengths, development needs and career direction — before they reach your recruitment process.

03

Workforce Readiness Programs

AACP™ delivers structured 8-week cohort programs and the RPAS Workforce Hub — purpose-built programming grounded in career intelligence and real aviation and aerospace industry context.

04

Sector-Level Intelligence

Beyond individual participants, AACP™ builds aggregated workforce intelligence across cohorts and programs — supporting workforce planning, policy development and sector strategy.

05

RPAS Industry Applications

The RPAS Workforce Hub addresses the growing demand for RPAS-capable workforce across infrastructure inspection, environmental monitoring, mapping and emergency management.

06

Canadian Aviation Focus

AACP™ is purpose-built for the Canadian aviation and aerospace sector — informed by Canadian aviation industry standards and grounded in Alberta's growing aerospace and RPAS ecosystem.
