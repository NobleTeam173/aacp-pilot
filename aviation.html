Career Pathways

Four High-Demand Career Pathways

Canada's aviation and aerospace sector spans four distinct career areas. AACP™ delivers the career intelligence you need to identify where your strengths fit and take confident next steps.

Pathway 01

Air Traffic Control

Directing aircraft safely through Canadian airspace — a high-precision career built on sustained attention and rapid decision-making.

Pathway 02

Aircraft Maintenance Engineering

Maintaining, inspecting and certifying aircraft systems — a technical discipline demanding procedural precision and mechanical expertise.

Pathway 03

Pilot — Flight Operations

Commercial, regional and charter flight careers requiring spatial reasoning, adaptive judgment and effective crew communication.

Pathway 04

Aerospace STEM

Engineering, avionics, software and emerging aerospace roles across aircraft systems design, manufacturing and UAV technology.
