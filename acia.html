ACIA™ Workforce Intelligence

Discover What Your
Capabilities Could Mean

ACIA™ Career Intelligence helps participants better understand their transferable strengths, potential career direction and practical next steps across aviation and aerospace — without predicting career success or suitability.

What ACIA™ Provides

Career Intelligence for
Informed Direction

ACIA™ is not a qualification pathway, an employment test or a prediction of career outcomes. It is a career intelligence experience designed to help participants better understand themselves and their potential direction in aviation and aerospace.

01

Capability Discovery

Through a structured series of missions, ACIA™ observes how participants approach problems, make decisions and engage with aviation and aerospace scenarios — translating those observations into a Workforce Profile.

02

Transferable Strengths

ACIA™ identifies capabilities that are transferable across aviation, aerospace and RPAS contexts — strengths you bring from any background that may be relevant to your career direction.

03

Career Exploration

Your Workforce Profile maps your observed capabilities against aviation and aerospace career pathways — helping you explore where your strengths may fit, based on what ACIA™ actually observed.

04

Personalized Workforce Profile

Every participant receives a structured, personalized Workforce Profile summarizing their identified strengths, development areas and potential pathway alignment — with practical next-step guidance.

Two Distinct Experiences

General ACIA™ and RPAS ACIA™
Are Separate Assessments

ACIA™ is tailored to the participant's pathway. The General ACIA™ experience and the RPAS ACIA™ experience are not interchangeable — each is designed for its specific workforce context.

General ACIA™

Aviation & Aerospace Career Intelligence

Designed for participants exploring aviation and aerospace careers broadly — including ATC, AME, Pilot and Aerospace STEM pathways. Available as a standalone assessment or as part of the 8-Week Workforce Readiness Cohort.

  • Covers aviation and aerospace career pathways
  • Structured career intelligence experience
  • Personalized Career Intelligence Report
  • Pathway alignment across aviation and aerospace
  • Available as individual purchase ($199–$399 CAD)
RPAS ACIA™

RPAS Workforce Intelligence

Designed specifically for the RPAS Workforce Hub. RPAS ACIA™ uses dedicated scenarios, components and missions grounded in remotely piloted aviation — and is distinct from the General ACIA™ experience.

  • Dedicated RPAS scenarios and missions
  • RPAS-specific career intelligence throughout
  • Workforce Profile focused on RPAS-relevant capabilities
  • RPAS career and application alignment
  • Included in the RPAS Workforce Hub ($1,200 CAD)
Important Context

What ACIA™ Is Not

ACIA™ is designed to provide career intelligence, not to evaluate fitness for specific roles or programs.

Not a qualification pathway

Not a Training Outcome

ACIA™ does not replace, lead to or substitute for any formal aviation training, qualification, medical standard or industry credential. It is a career intelligence experience, not a training program.

Not a selection tool

Not an Employment Test

ACIA™ results are not shared with employers or used for hiring decisions. Your Workforce Profile belongs to you.

Not a prediction

Not a Career Guarantee

ACIA™ helps you better understand your capabilities and potential direction — it does not predict career success or employment outcomes.
